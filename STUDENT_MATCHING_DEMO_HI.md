# Student Skills & Job Matching Demo (Hinglish)

1. Run `py -m pip install -r requirements.txt` inside `rozgarsetu-engine`.
2. Run `py app.py` and open `http://127.0.0.1:5000`.
3. Open **Career Twin** from the left sidebar.
4. In **Student Skills Profile**, edit skills (default: Python, SQL, HTML, Git) and click **Save Skills & Find Matching Jobs**.
5. The app opens **Job Matches** and compares the skills against saved company job requirements.
6. To explore sample MSMEs, open **Rojgar Setu (Recruiter)** and use **MSME Company Directory**. Their sample openings also show in Job Matches (demo only).
7. Platform job matching only uses requirements posted by a company account in this application. External companies in the directory are not platform partners, and their vacancies are not automatically imported.

The student skill form is a presentation profile stored in this browser's local storage; applying to a job inside this platform still requires a logged-in student account. For real openings, verify eligibility and apply through the official company careers page.
