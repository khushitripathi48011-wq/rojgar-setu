# MSME Company Directory (Sample)

The recruiter panel now lists 12 **sample MSME companies** from Uttar Pradesh instead of large MNCs. Each has one sample opening (role, job type and required skills).

- These are demo profiles, not registered or verified employers. Names are illustrative.
- Their sample openings appear in **Job Matches** with a "SAMPLE MSME OPENING" label and cannot be applied to.
- Real MSMEs register via the recruiter panel; their posted jobs appear first in Job Matches and can be applied to.
- The list lives in `MSME_COMPANY_DIRECTORY` in `app.py`; edit it to change companies or skills.
